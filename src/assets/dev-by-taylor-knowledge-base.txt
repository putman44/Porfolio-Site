Dev by Taylor Knowledge Base

Business Overview:
Dev by Taylor builds websites, web applications, dashboards, backend systems, automations, and AI-powered tools for small businesses, startups, and technical teams.

Services:
1. Website Development
Custom portfolio sites, business websites, landing pages, service pages, and responsive frontend builds.

2. Web Applications
Full-stack applications using React, TypeScript, Python, Flask, Node, PostgreSQL, and related tools.

3. Automation
Workflow automation using tools like n8n, APIs, webhooks, CRMs, email systems, and custom scripts.

4. AI Agents and Chatbots
Lead generation chatbots, internal assistants, AI workflow tools, GPT-powered forms, customer support assistants, and data extraction workflows.

5. Dashboards and Data Tools
Custom dashboards for business data, operations, reporting, and process visibility.

6. Backend/API Development
REST APIs, authentication, database design, integrations, and backend services.

Process:
A typical project starts with a discovery conversation to understand the business problem, users, timeline, and budget. After that, Taylor recommends a practical MVP, builds the first version, tests it, and helps deploy it.

Good Fit Projects:
Good projects include business websites, custom internal tools, dashboards, automations, AI lead capture systems, CRM workflows, and MVP web applications.

Timeline:
Small website or automation projects can often start with a simple MVP. Larger apps or dashboard systems require more planning and phased development.

Pricing:
Pricing depends on scope. Simple landing pages or automations are smaller projects. Full-stack applications, AI workflows, and dashboards require more time and planning. The chatbot should collect project type, description, timeline, budget, and contact information before Taylor gives a specific estimate.

Contact:
Interested users should provide their name, email, company, website, project description, timeline, and budget so Taylor can follow up.
